# Meridian Health · Group Coverage Agreement
Policy reference: MH-GC-2027-1048
Effective period: January 01, 2027 – December 31, 2027
Prepared for: Northstar Services (demonstration only)

## 01. Coverage overview
This agreement establishes a group medical benefits framework for eligible employees and dependents. The plan includes outpatient consultations, hospitalization, emergency care and selected preventive services. Benefits are subject to the plan schedule and applicable exclusions.
The annual aggregate benefit ceiling is INR 500,000 per enrolled family. Network hospital treatment may be handled through cashless authorization, while non-network care is reviewed for reimbursement.

## 02. Eligibility and enrollment
Employees actively working at least 30 hours per week are eligible for coverage. Spouses and dependent children up to age 25 may be enrolled when supporting records are provided. Enrollment must be completed within 30 calendar days of joining the organization.
Changes resulting from marriage, birth or qualifying loss of other coverage should be reported within 30 days. Late enrollment requests require review and may be subject to waiting periods.

## 03. Benefits and limits
Inpatient hospitalization is covered up to the available annual family limit, subject to medical necessity and policy conditions. Emergency transport is covered up to INR 8,000 per event. Outpatient diagnostics are covered up to INR 25,000 per family per policy year.
Certain procedures require preauthorization. Room category restrictions and procedure-level sublimits, where applicable, are defined in the benefit schedule. No benefit amount in this document should be interpreted outside its specified conditions.

## 04. Claims and reimbursements
Network hospitals should initiate preauthorization before planned admissions. For emergency admissions, notification should be submitted as soon as practicable, ordinarily within 24 hours. Reimbursement claims must include itemized invoices, discharge summaries, prescriptions and supporting diagnostic records.
Complete reimbursement claims should be submitted within 60 calendar days after discharge. Incomplete claims may be placed on hold until documentation is received. Appeals must be made within 30 days of the adverse determination.

## 05. Exclusions and exceptions
Non-medically necessary procedures, purely cosmetic treatments, experimental therapies and services specifically excluded in the benefit schedule are not covered. Pre-existing condition treatment may be subject to the waiting periods stated in the plan schedule.
Exceptions are considered only when explicitly endorsed in writing. Verbal representations do not amend the policy. Any ambiguity is resolved by reference to the executed contract and incorporated schedules.

## 06. Data protection and confidentiality
Personal and health-related information must be used only for approved eligibility, claims and service operations. Access is limited to authorized personnel with a legitimate need. Transfers to service providers must be governed by appropriate contractual safeguards.
Security incidents affecting protected records should be escalated promptly through the designated incident response process. The parties will cooperate on legally required notifications and remediation.

## 07. Renewal and termination
The agreement expires on December 31, 2027 unless renewed in writing. Renewal terms, pricing and benefits are subject to mutual written agreement. Either party may terminate for material breach following written notice and an opportunity to remedy as specified in the executed agreement.
On termination, outstanding valid claims remain subject to the contract terms applicable at the date of service. Records are retained or securely disposed of in accordance with applicable retention requirements.
